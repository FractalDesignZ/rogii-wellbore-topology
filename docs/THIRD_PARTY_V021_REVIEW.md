# Review of the supplied v0.2.1 archive

This is a source review of `1-rogii-wellbore-topology-v0.2.1-updated.zip`. Archive tests and benchmark results were not executed as part of this review. Its reported **1.5019 ft** result describes a 16-well **synthetic StarSteer** cohort, not the real 773-well ROGII cohort (`docs/UPGRADE_V02_CHANGELOG.md:22-35`; `scripts/benchmark_upgrade_ablation.py:23,35-50`). The archive contains no retained ablation report or per-row OOF predictions for independently checking that number.

All paths and line numbers below refer to files inside the supplied archive.

Wide structural-coordinate alignment in `S = TVT + Z`, posterior uncertainty, and coverage-aware cohort metrics remain useful candidates. They require individual review and controlled measurement. The archive is retained as an isolated reference rather than applied over the public implementation.

A separate private exploratory track may test the reviewed pure alignment mathematics with fixed archive defaults, no shock/dynamics provider, sanitized lateral inputs, retained predictions, and labels accessed only after prediction artifacts are saved. Such a result is new pilot evidence, not validation of the archive's synthetic number or its full pipeline.

The first isolated real-data attempt was rejected: only 51,128 of 54,833 pilot rows were finite, and its matched finite-subset RMSE was 62.860124 ft versus last-known 11.891860 ft. This is not a full-cohort score. A valid smooth Z crossing through `-9999.25` exposed an inherited coordinate-null decoding error; the resulting NaN contaminated one well's posterior. The public reader now preserves finite coordinates unless their source declares a null code. That ingestion repair receives a fresh fixed-recipe attempt; it does not retroactively validate or overwrite the rejected run.

The corrected-ingestion attempt also rejects the candidate: all 54,833 rows are finite, with RMSE **60.817223 ft** versus last-known **11.616770 ft**. All 11 previously finite wells remain bit-identical; only the affected well regains predictions. Parameters, mathematical alignment, donor/shock settings and fallbacks were unchanged. This controlled real-data result does not reproduce the archive's synthetic accuracy claim.
